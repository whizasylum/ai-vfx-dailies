SuperSplat Editor 3.0 ships with major performance overhaul for large Gaussian splats

A free, browser-based splat editor that scales to tens of millions of points is a real triage tool for splat-heavy pipelines.

PlayCanvas released version 3.0 of SuperSplat, its free browser-based Gaussian splat viewer and editor. The update lets users visualize and edit tens of millions of Gaussians smoothly, a substantial jump from prior versions. It remains free and web-based, meaning no local install or licence cost for reviewing or trimming splat captures before bringing them into a production pipeline.

Rising Sun Pictures' REVIZE face and body replacement tool rolled out across sister studios

Shows an in-house VFX ML tool maturing into a shared, multi-studio production pipeline rather than a one-off.

Pitch Black is deploying REVIZE, the machine-learning face- and body-replacement pipeline Rising Sun Pictures has developed since 2018 and used on 30+ films including Sinners and Mickey 17, across FOLKS, El Ranchito and Square One. Troy Tobin, a longtime RSP leader, is running the program. Details on the underlying tech stack, licensing, or availability outside the Pitch Black group are not disclosed.

LTX-2.5 22B gets an IC-LoRA for video colorization and clean-plate work

A VFX-tagged LoRA on an open video model is worth a look, but licence terms and real-world quality are unverified.

Lightricks published an IC-LoRA checkpoint for its LTX-2.5-22b video model targeting colorization, clean-plate generation, and object removal, tagged explicitly for VFX use. The model is gated on Hugging Face under a custom licence requiring acceptance of terms, and adoption so far is minimal (1 like, 35 downloads). No independent verification of quality or performance is available yet.
