R 01

Wan 2.7 ships with open weights and camera control

First controllable video model you can legally run on a client show.

Alibaba released Wan 2.7 under Apache 2.0 with first/last frame conditioning and explicit camera control. Weights are on Hugging Face and ComfyUI nodes shipped the same day. Vendor figures put 1080p at 24GB VRAM, untested independently. The permissive licence is the notable part: unlike most of the field this is usable on client work without a commercial tier.
