Chaos Corona 16 adds relighting of Gaussian Splats for 3ds Max and Cinema 4D

Chaos released Corona 16, adding support for relighting 3D Gaussian Splats directly inside the renderer, plus a new parallax interiors system for architectural visualization. This is one of the first mainstream commercial renderers to treat splats as a relightable asset type rather than a static capture, signalling splats are moving from capture format toward a first-class renderable asset.

WHY IT MATTERS

Shows splats becoming a lit, renderable asset type in commercial DCC renderers, not just a viewer format.
