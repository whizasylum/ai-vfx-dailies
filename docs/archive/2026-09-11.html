Lightricks releases a batch of task-specific IC-LoRAs for LTX-2.5

Task-specific relighting and restoration LoRAs on an open video model are close to production-usable if licence terms hold.

Lightricks published six IC-LoRA adapters for its LTX-2.5-22b video model on Hugging Face: day-to-night relighting, pixel spatial upscaling, water simulation, ingredient/reference-sheet character consistency, video decompression/restoration, and a cinemagraph selective-motion LoRA. All are gated under LTX's own licence, distributed as diffusion-single-file weights. Download counts vary wildly (upscaler at ~5,900, others under 10), suggesting most just landed. No word yet on ComfyUI node support.

MiniMax-H3-Turbo image-to-video model hits Apache-2.0 on Hugging Face

A genuinely open-licence, high-adoption video model is usable on client work without licensing risk.

lightx2v's MiniMax-H3-Turbo, an i2v/t2v/r2v model built on MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, is available under Apache-2.0 via diffusers. It has racked up 886 likes and over 1.3 million downloads since early August, indicating real community uptake rather than a fresh drop. Permissive licence and diffusers support make it straightforward to slot into existing pipelines.

Maxon previews fall Cinema 4D, ZBrush and Redshift updates at IBC2026

Worth a calendar note for October, but nothing to act on until the actual release lands.

Maxon will show early looks at its October ecosystem release at IBC (Sept 11-14), including new procedural 3D world-building in Cinema 4D, a desktop-and-iPad sculpting workflow for ZBrush, procedural fur and colour updates in Redshift, and AI-powered relighting in its effects suite. Details are preview-stage only; no ship date, licensing, or technical specifics given beyond marketing copy.
