B 02

Vicon acquires facial capture firm Captive Devices

Affects vendor roadmap and support for facial capture hardware used alongside MetaHuman pipelines.

Vicon, the Oxford Metrics motion capture division, has acquired Captive Devices, the Manchester-based maker of the CoreHMC head-mounted capture rig. CoreHMC feeds performer facial data into Unreal Engine and MetaHuman Animator workflows. This consolidates a facial mocap hardware vendor into a major mocap supplier, which could affect pricing, support, and integration roadmaps for studios using either system.
