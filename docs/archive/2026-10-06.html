From Idea to Game in ONE Day with AI - Fastest Character Workflow

A 'split-and-assemble' workflow for AI characters that avoids the 'blob' topology of single-mesh generation by creating limbs, head, and gear as separate objects with controlled polycounts.

WHY IT MATTERS

This workflow bridges the gap between raw AI generation and production-ready assets by inserting a Blender assembly stage. In a USD pipeline, these components would be brought in as separate prims. The vision-agent texture extraction could be replicated in a Python-based Houdini COPs or Nuke script.

  • Tripo P2.0
  • Mr. Mak Workspace
  • Blender
  • Mixamo
  • Modif
Inside the workflow · 4 takeaways
  • Dividing complex character designs into discrete components for individual AI mesh generation to improve topological fidelity.
  • Using vision-language agents (GPT/Claude) to derive PBR maps (Normal, Roughness, Metallic) directly from a generated 2D color texture.
  • Prompting AI models to ignore lighting during texture generation to create flatter, more usable base-color maps.
